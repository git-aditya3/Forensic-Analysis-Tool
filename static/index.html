Examination protocol

The core system follows a bounded, auditable path.

  1. 01
    AcquireCopy source bytes and calculate MD5 / SHA-256.
  2. 02
    IdentifyRoute by recorder signatures without mounting media.
  3. 03
    RecoverParse native blocks or carve surviving streams.
  4. 04
    ReportExport ranges, hashes, audit trail, and findings.
Every action is appended to a hash-linked custody chain.
